✅ Here Are Powerful & Practical Feature Ideas to Enhance NotificationsBell.jsx:
🔔 1. Notification Types & Icons
Add categorization to alerts:

🔴 Critical: Spending exceeded by large margin (e.g., >150% of threshold)

🟡 Warning: Slightly over budget (e.g., >100% but <150%)

🟢 Info: Budget approaching threshold (e.g., 80–100%)

Benefit: Visual prioritization for users.

🗓️ 2. Schedule-Based Alerts
Allow users to set:

Recurring monthly limit warnings

Weekly expense summaries

📉 3. Spike Detection
Detect sudden spending surges and notify:

"⚠️ Your spending in 'Takeout' spiked 250% compared to last week."

Uses: calculate % change from previous time period per category.

🔄 4. Editable Alerts
Right now, users can delete alerts. But allow:

✏️ Inline editing (change threshold or subcategory)

Save changes instantly with a backend update

🧠 5. AI-generated Alert Suggestions
Let chatbot or system suggest:

“Would you like to set an alert for 'Coffee'? You've spent $92 this month.”

Uses pattern recognition to recommend new alerts.

🧾 6. Alert History / Log
Allow users to toggle past alerts they've received:

“View past exceeded notifications for this month”

This can be a tab under notifications.

📊 7. Smart Summarized View
For multiple exceeded alerts:

Combine similar messages into 1 smart message:

“⚠️ You've exceeded thresholds in 3 categories: Food, Transport, and Entertainment.”

Expandable for full details.

📍 8. Geo-tagged Notifications (if GPS/spending source available)
“⚠️ Spent $120 on Restaurants in Austin last weekend.”

(Not immediately feasible unless integrated with a GPS-enabled receipt scanner or card data.)

🎯 9. Goal-based Reminders
Let users set targets, not just thresholds:

“🎯 You’ve reached 60% of your savings goal for this month!”

Alerts them when they’re progressing toward positive goals too.

📅 10. Snooze or Mute Alerts
Allow users to snooze an alert for a time:

“Remind me later”

“Don’t alert for this category this week”
